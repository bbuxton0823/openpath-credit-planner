# Research ledger for OpenPath

**Date:** October 4, 2026. **Scope:** Peralta college credit planning for high-school dual-enrollment students pursuing first-year entry, with a connected OUSD graduation-credit outcome under review. OpenPath is a provisional name for a local prototype.

## Provenance and limits

Evidence layers are distinguished throughout this file:

- **Prior session research, supplied October 4, 2026:** the course-level ASSIST checks, interactive A&T database queries, HBCU policy review, and competitor review were provided in the project handoff. They were not all repeated while writing these files. The handoff is a research record, not an independently captured archive of the original pages.
- **Reopened in this build, October 4, 2026:** public pages specifically marked below were retrieved and their readable content inspected during documentation. A successful page opening does not independently repeat a prior interactive database query.
- **Coordinating OUSD research, supplied October 4, 2026:** the district baseline, policy bodies, dates, external-course memo, and school findings in section 7 were supplied by the coordinating research pass. This documentation pass independently reopened the district graduation page, Skyline, Oakland Technical, Rudsdale, the district dual-enrollment page, and its linked public course sheet. Reopening the board-policy URLs failed here, so those findings remain attributed to the coordinating pass.

Academic effective year, source publication date, and retrieval date are different fields. A page retrieved in October 2026 is not automatically 2026-27 evidence. Links are provided for inspection, but source availability can change. No college was contacted, no student record was submitted, and no account or paid service was used.

### First-year route and degree-application boundary

[UC first-year requirements](https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/admission-requirements/first-year-requirements/) was **reopened in this build**. It confirms that college coursework taken during high school and through the summer after graduation does not alone change the student to a transfer applicant. Its campus directory also lists the nine undergraduate destinations used in the prototype.

[UC dual-enrollment guidance](https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/counselors/preparing-freshman-students/dual-enrollment.html) was **reopened in this build**. It states that transferable dual-enrollment credit contributes to degree units and that official college transcripts are required for admitted students. It distinguishes the transfer-oriented Cal-GETC pattern from first-year expectations. The prototype therefore must not turn UC-E, UC-M, or Cal-GETC labels into proof of first-year campus GE/major completion. It also must not describe a completed course as satisfying grade-dependent rules without evaluating those conditions.

No campus-specific first-year GE or major requirement was verified in the supplied research or this documentation pass. No 60-unit transfer, TAG, or CCC-HBCU transfer guarantee is a first-year eligibility rule in this MVP.

## 5. Evidence vocabulary used by the prototype

| Label | Meaning | Must not imply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Published unit-credit evidence | An official source lists the course within a defined scope/year | A student's final award or a campus GE/major match |
| Preliminary equivalency | A public source names a possible destination equivalent subject to review | Official award, current effective year, or degree applicability |
| Year mismatch | Student course year differs from the evidence year | Automatic rejection or acceptance in the newer year |
| Year not stated | The source gives no effective year | That the retrieval year is the effective year |
| Review required | A condition, discrepancy, or applicability question is unresolved | A negative outcome |
| No verified match | The seed research does not establish this specific course-destination relationship | The course will not transfer |
| Lookup not completed | Access or research limits prevented the course query | A searched database with zero results |
| GE/major not verified | No applicable requirement-level evidence was captured | That no credit can be useful |

Retrieval failure and missing policy applicability belong in provenance. They must not be erased by a confidence score. No language model or judgment API may fill these evidence gaps by similarity.

## 8. Official OUSD Fall 2026 schedule snapshot

The [OUSD dual-enrollment page](https://www.ousd.org/high-school-linked-learning-office/for-students-families/dual-enrollment) was reopened on October 4, 2026. Its participating-high-schools link leads to the public [All DE Courses sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/14_D4lvEDjvou_TBa6q5StlEB8zTiK5lsvI52qjDwGZU/edit?usp=sharing). The linked sheet was opened and its public [CSV export](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/14_D4lvEDjvou_TBa6q5StlEB8zTiK5lsvI52qjDwGZU/export?format=csv) read for this one-time snapshot. The app has no spreadsheet connection or runtime fetch.

`src/ousd-schedule.js` preserves all **79 populated course rows**, source rows 2 through 80: 74 Peralta listings and five CSU East Bay listings. They cover 16 normalized school/program groups. Original school labels and source-row numbers remain available for provenance. Repeated listings and sections are retained; this is full coverage of the reviewed tab, not 79 distinct course identities or a claim about every OUSD offering.

Only public course fields were imported: school, source college, published code/title, units text, source term, pathway, transfer flag, and source reference. No staff names, contact details, student records, class locations, enrollment-management fields, or private-sheet contents are included. A source row's term is retained even when it says trimester. The dataset groups the snapshot as Fall 2026 without asserting the student's actual attendance term.

| Curation choice | Treatment and evidence limit |
| --- | --- |
| College identity | Peralta rows use the existing college IDs. Five CSU East Bay rows retain their college name with a null supported-college ID and are reference-only. |
| Exact course identity | Only Merritt ENGL C1000 exactly matches the original researched college/code records. No fuzzy renumbering bridge is allowed. The four published `PSYCH C1000` rows do not inherit `PSYC C1000` evidence. |
| Numeric units | Nine combined, alternative, ranged or variable-unit rows retain `units: null` and original `unitsText`. Source rows are 9, 23, 24, 26, 27, 31, 78, 79 and 80. Unknown does not mean zero. |
| Codes with extra notes | The first published line remains the course label; prerequisite/corequisite notes are retained separately. Combined codes and section labels are preserved instead of split into invented individual courses. |
| Addable identities | The integration can create neutral records for unambiguous supported Peralta codes. Ambiguous combined/range/section codes remain review-only. A schedule identity does not inherit historical title/units or destination evidence without an exact supported match. |
| Transfer column | Header is `UC/CSU transfer (OUSD Transcript codes TBD)`. Preserve the public flag as a district schedule statement. It is neither a high-school subject award nor a verified university requirement/equivalence. |

Two important counterexamples are preserved: Bunche's Laney ART 205 listing has 2 semester units and is marked not transferable; Castlemont's Merritt RLEST 2A is marked CSU Only. The district's broad DE guidance must not overwrite a specific listed limitation.

The schedule supplies no per-student approval, enrollment, open-seat guarantee, final award, verified HBCU match, or first-year GE/major match. It does not refresh the separate ASSIST 2025-26 evidence. A saved course must still carry a student-confirmed status and term/year. Additional research is needed to resolve ambiguous identities and update offerings after the snapshot date.
